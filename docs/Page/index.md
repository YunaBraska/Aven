<section class="section">
  <h2>A conversation that does not take over the screen.</h2>
  <p class="section-intro">Aven keeps spoken prompts, typed input and dropped files in one ordered conversation while your attention remains in the app where the work is happening.</p>
  <div class="feature-grid">
    <article class="feature"><h3>Speak when you choose</h3><p>Press and hold your recorded shortcut to speak. Release it to transcribe and send a prompt without a wake word or permanent listening.</p></article>
    <article class="feature"><h3>Keep the thread</h3><p>Continue a conversation through the current project instead of restarting the context for every follow-up.</p></article>
    <article class="feature"><h3>Record with consent</h3><p>Meeting recording starts only after confirmation. A visible recording state stays present while capture is active.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>Built for deliberate access.</h2>
  <p class="section-intro">Aven keeps credentials in its own Keychain service, asks before sending a conversation through the installed Codex client and does not add its own telemetry.</p>
  <p class="actions"><a class="button secondary" href="privacy.html">Read the privacy details</a></p>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>Availability</h2>
  <p class="section-intro">Aven supports macOS 14 and later. It is still under active development, so there is no supported public download yet.</p>
