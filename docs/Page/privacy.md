<section class="page-heading">
  <p class="eyebrow">Privacy</p>
  <h1>Voice input stays deliberate.</h1>
  <p>Aven is a local macOS app. It does not use a wake word or an always-listening microphone.</p>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>What Aven handles</h2>
  <div class="feature-grid">
    <article class="feature"><h3>Voice and meetings</h3><p>Microphone use starts when you hold the configured talk key. Meeting capture requires an explicit confirmation. Raw meeting audio is not retained by Aven.</p></article>
    <article class="feature"><h3>Local app data</h3><p>Preferences, project associations and app-owned records stay on the Mac. Credentials are stored in Aven's dedicated macOS Keychain service.</p></article>
    <article class="feature"><h3>Codex conversations</h3><p>Aven asks for forwarding consent before it starts a Codex process. The installed Codex client then handles the conversation under its own account and service terms.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>What Aven does not add</h2>
  <p class="section-intro">Aven does not add its own analytics or telemetry. It does not enumerate unrelated Keychain entries.</p>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>Questions and reports</h2>
  <p class="section-intro">For a privacy or security concern, use the project's responsible disclosure channel. Do not publish credentials, private transcripts or audio in a public issue.</p>
  <p class="actions"><a class="button secondary" href="https://github.com/YunaBraska/Aven/security/policy">Security policy</a></p>
</section>
